Privacy Policy

Make Them Bleed: Voxel Carnage · CampStudio
Effective date: November 18, 2026
Android package: com.campstudio.makethembleed

This policy explains how CampStudio handles information when you play Make Them Bleed: Voxel Carnage (the “game”), contact us, or visit this policy page.

There is no CampStudio account or login. Progress, coins and settings are saved on your device. CampStudio does not run its own analytics or servers for this game. The game uses Google AdMob for advertising and Google Play billing for the optional NO ADS purchase.

1. Information handled by the game

  • Local game data: progress, unlocked levels and moves, coins, settings, control layout and the NO ADS entitlement are stored in the app's storage on your device. CampStudio does not operate a cloud-save service for this game and does not receive this data. Android backup or device-transfer features may preserve app data according to your device settings.
  • Advertising information: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may process identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, device and app information, advertising interactions and diagnostics. The data processed depends on your device settings, region and the advertising features available in your version. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, detect fraud and maintain its services.
  • Payments: the optional NO ADS purchase is handled by Google Play under its own policies and integrated through Unity In-App Purchasing. Records related to it may include the product, price, currency, entitlement and purchase receipt. CampStudio does not receive your payment-card details.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to send. Please do not include passwords or payment-card details.

The game does not provide chat, does not send push notifications and does not ask for access to your contacts, microphone, camera, photos or precise location.

2. Why information is used

Local data supports gameplay, settings, rewards and the NO ADS entitlement. Advertising services deliver and measure ads (including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch) and help prevent abuse. Payment providers process information to complete purchases, for payment security and legal obligations. Support information is used to answer your request.

3. Services that receive information

  • Google AdMob and Google Play: these services process advertising and payment information under Google's Privacy Policy. See also Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.
  • Unity In-App Purchasing: the game uses this SDK to connect to Google Play Billing, retrieve the price and entitlement of NO ADS and acknowledge the purchase. Depending on the enabled Unity services, the SDK may also process purchase, installation and diagnostic information. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: this policy is hosted by GitHub. Visiting the page sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address, to GitHub. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. This page has no advertising, analytics scripts or third-party fonts added by CampStudio.

Information may be processed outside your country by these services. Their policies explain their processing, retention and international-transfer practices. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

4. Your choices

  • Advertising: where required, the game asks for your advertising choices through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. You can use Android's privacy and advertising controls to reset or delete your advertising ID and manage advertising preferences. Google's My Ad Center provides additional controls where supported. Buying NO ADS removes the banner and the interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads stay available as your choice.
  • Purchases: Google Play returns an owned NO ADS purchase when the game is reinstalled with the same Google account. Reinstalling or erasing data does not refund a purchase.
  • Local progress: to remove all local game data, use Android Settings → Apps → Make Them Bleed → Storage → Clear storage/data, or uninstall the game. This permanently erases your progress. Device backups may separately restore data.

5. Retention, deletion and privacy requests

Local game data remains until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to device backup and restore. CampStudio holds no gameplay data for this game on its own servers. Support emails are retained as needed to resolve requests and for applicable legal requirements.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict processing, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact CampStudio using the address below to make a request. Requests about information held independently by Google, Unity or GitHub should also be directed to those providers. Erasing game data or uninstalling does not delete Google's purchase history, issue a refund or cancel a transaction.

6. Age and children

The game contains intense cartoon violence and blood and is intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information can contact us to request help and deletion where applicable.

7. Security

The game sends no gameplay data to CampStudio. The advertising and payment services described above use encrypted connections. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
